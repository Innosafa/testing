About Our Hotel

A Home Away From Home in Makueni County

KIBWEZI EASTVIEW provides guests with a comfortable and welcoming environment for business, leisure, dining, and overnight accommodation.

We proudly operate two locations: our Kibwezi Branch along the Kibwezi–Kitui Road (rooms at KES 1,100, 1,500, and 2,000), and our second branch in Wote along the Machakos–Wote Road near Kaiti River (rooms at KES 2,500 and 3,500).

  • Canopy Beds with Mosquito Netting
  • Fresh Food, Tilapia & Buffet Catering
  • Attentive 24/7 Guest Reception
  • Free Wi-Fi & Secure Parking
