The 5-Point Transformation Agenda for Kikumbulyu South

Directly transcribed from Hon. John Nyamai's official campaign blueprint. Five measurable commitments to restore dignity, water, healthcare, and economic growth.

01
Improved Roads Infrastructure

Feeder Roads, Culverts & Concrete Drifts

Comprehensive grading of over 120km of farm-to-market feeder roads connecting rural farms to Kambu and Machinery. Construction of permanent concrete drifts across seasonal streams and high-mast solar lights in all trading centers.

02
Universal Water Access

Solar-Powered Boreholes & Piped Distribution

Solarization of community boreholes to eliminate high fuel tariffs, desilting earth dams in Utithi and Masongaleni, and extending piping to schools, dispensaries, and village water kiosks.

03
Better Healthcare Access

Stocked Dispensaries & 24/7 Maternity Solarization

Consistent medicine supply in all ward dispensaries, upgraded maternity wings with solar backup power, and community health promoter stipends to ensure no mother or child is left behind.

04
Youth and Women Empowerment

Hustler Fund, Modern Boda Boda Sheds & Sports

Constructing weatherproof boda boda sheds with phone charging ports, seed capital and table banking for women chamas, and the annual "Kidero Super Cup" to nurture grassroots soccer talent.

05
Education Support

Transparent Ward Bursaries & Modern ECDE Classrooms

100% digitized, transparent ward bursary allocation prioritizing orphans and vulnerable students, model ECDE classrooms, and TVET sponsorships at Machinery Vocational Training Centre.
